Add it to your site

Upload the files to your site's root folder, then paste this into the page's <head> (for the tab icon) and header.

<!-- in <head> -->
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon-32.png" sizes="32x32" type="image/png">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">

<!-- in your header -->
<a href="/"><img src="/vantage-logo.svg" alt="Vantage" height="40"></a>

<!-- on a dark background -->
<img src="/vantage-logo-white.svg" alt="Vantage" height="40">

Using Squarespace, Wix or WordPress? Upload vantage-logo.png as the site logo and favicon-32.png as the favicon or browser icon.

Guidelines

SizeSet the logo to 32–48px tall in a header. Don't use it below 24px; switch to the mark only below that.
Clear spaceKeep empty space around the logo at least the height of the triangle inside the V.
BackgroundsUse gunmetal on white or light grey. Silver looks best on near-black (#16181c) or dark photos.
Don'tStretch it, recolour it, add effects, or rearrange the mark and the name.
